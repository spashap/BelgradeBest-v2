  - Search 350, mostly Bing and engines that use Bing's results.
  - Direct 153, AI assistants 21 (67% engaged and growing fastest), Facebook 15.
  - Google effectively sends nothing.
- **Bing:** about 290 appearances a day, a record. The click rate fell to 2.5% this week; one cause (a snippet sentence on the Expo hub) was fixed on 27 Nov.

**The honest headline:** no free, proven tactic turns 548 visits a month into thousands by January. Growth will come mostly from rising Expo demand and real Expo news. The work below makes sure we capture that demand and turn more of it into bookings. Expect dozens of extra qualified visits from it, not hundreds.

## Progress

  - **The data:** 763 Bing appearances over 90 days on the arrival pages, grouped by what the searcher wanted to do:

    | Task | Appearances | Clicks |
    |---|---|---|
    | Taxi fares and the voucher | 232 | 10 |
    | Generic "airport to city centre" | 180 | 9 |
    | Bus 72 or the A1 minibus | 176 | 9 |
    | Train stations | 105 | 4 |
    | Distance and travel time | 26 | 1 |
    | Arriving at night | 2 | 0 |

  - **Coverage:**
    - The airport page already answers every one of these tasks with November 2026 facts: a comparison table, how to pay, the voucher zones, touts, ride-hail apps, night buses, the station link and the airport railway.
    - The stations page already answers "which is the main train station" (Beograd Centar / Prokop) for its top queries.
  - **Decision:** the limit is clicks on queries where we already rank 2nd to 4th, and snippets stay frozen until 25 Dec. So no content change was made.
  - **Next:** measure against the target (25 airport clicks by 31 Jan) and look at the snippet again after 25 Dec only if clicks lag. The Budapest-train line on the stations page is due its own check on 13 Dec, the announced launch date.

## Not doing (and why)

- **Email ticket alert.** Already rejected in November: about 2–5 subscribers in 6 months, and the law needs a postal address and a named controller, which conflicts with the owner's privacy. My draft brought it back by mistake; Codex caught it.
- **New German pages.** German demand is almost all tickets, opening hours, coach trips and "how many days". The German airport, getting-there and stay pages already cover it. Reopen only if an uncovered topic reaches 100 appearances in 4 weeks.
- **Evergreen rewrites** (currency, best time to visit, safety, things to do). We already rank 1–5 for these; the volumes are tiny. Keep them accurate only.
- **A weekly "what changed" block on the hub.** Bing's ranking factors are relevance, credibility, engagement, freshness, language/location and speed. A weekly timestamp isn't a substantive update.
- **Bing Places** (needs a physical address), **MSN/Microsoft Start** (a publishing operation), **YouTube Shorts** (links in descriptions aren't clickable), **Tripadvisor forums** (self-promotion is banned), **Quora** (no evidence it sends travel visitors).
- **llms.txt as a growth lever.** No evidence it helps; it stays as it is.
- **Travel-agency outreach.** Package sellers mostly bundle their own hotels. Only answer if an agency writes.
- **Data pages, homepage work, food pages, Facebook effort.** None of them moved traffic.

The pages that link to it get few readers themselves.
