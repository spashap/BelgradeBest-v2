Expo tickets: the 15 Sep on-sale date passed — pages rewritten to say so

Verified on 17 September 2026 against the official programme page (EN
"Tickets available soon", SR "Ulaznice uskoro dostupne") and the Serbian
press: ticket sales did NOT open on 15 September. No launch, no prices, no
announcement of a delay, and no replacement date. Every page that promised
that date was therefore wrong as of two days ago.

What the rewrite adds, all verified:

- It is the SECOND slipped date. On 25 January 2026 Finance Minister Siniša
  Mali told a government session "on 15 May we start selling tickets in
  Serbia and around the world." That date passed too. Both are now in a
  dated table with their sources and outcomes.
- A new forward date exists, announced today: at the "Visit Serbia" business
  meeting in Bratislava, Slovak tourism representatives said Slovak travel
  agencies will sell packages including an Expo ticket from 1 November 2026
  (Tanjug and B92; Igor Kovačević of Expo 2027 d.o.o. took part). It is the
  first named third-party sales channel — and the strongest signal yet that
  real ticket inventory and prices exist by then, which the page labels as
  an inference, not a statement.
- Whether the tender's first print batch (160,000 + 40,000 by 14 September)
  was actually delivered has not been reported, so the cause of the slip is
  unknown and the page says so.
