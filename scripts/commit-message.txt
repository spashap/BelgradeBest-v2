Slovak pilot for Expo 2027, generalised language mirrors, language estimate

Slovak pilot (BB-CHG-0034): /sk/expo-2027 (a compact hub with the facts, the
1 Nov Slovak ticket packages, the Slovak pavilion and an FAQ), plus
/sk/expo-2027/tickets and /sk/expo-2027/getting-there, translated from the
English masters with identical facts and dates. Shipped now rather than on
1 Nov, because Bing took 3-4 weeks to crawl the German set.

The language mirrors are generalised in lib/i18n.ts: Lang adds "sk", with
TR_LANGS, UI[lang] chrome strings, PAGE_TWINS, langPath() and twinLangs().
alternates() now lists only the languages a page actually has, so the Expo
hub, tickets and getting-there carry en/de/sk hreflang (reciprocal), while
other German pages keep en/de. Header nav, footer note, TOC label, skip link,
LangNote, date locale, sitemap lastmod and the growth classifier all know
Slovak. The new `sk` content collection and /sk routes mirror /de.

The estimate is in growth/BELGRADEBEST_LANGUAGE_ESTIMATE.md: no full Russian
site, but a ~6-page Russian pilot after 1 Nov; Russian + Turkish rather than
Italian; Serbian only as B2B pages. CLAUDE.md is updated.

Known: tests/growth/changes.test.ts fails on a pre-existing id gap
(BB-CHG-0017..0019 were never written). The other 35 tests pass.
