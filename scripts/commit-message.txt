Footer: social icon links (Facebook, Reddit) with standard brand glyphs

The footer's social list was empty. It now carries the two live brand
accounts as round icon buttons at the end of the bottom bar: new
components/SocialIcon.astro holds the standard 24×24 brand glyphs
(Facebook, Reddit, X, Instagram, LinkedIn — currentColor, so tokens colour
them), SiteFooter renders an icon per entry with the label kept for screen
readers and as the tooltip, and globals.css adds c-footer__social plus a
u-visually-hidden helper. Entries live in site-config.json
chrome.footer.social with a new `icon` key (admin Navigation still toggles
and reorders them); the admin store type knows the field. X is added the
day that account exists.
