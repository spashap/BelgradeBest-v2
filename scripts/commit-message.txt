Homepage and leg hubs: fetch the stage photo first

After the inline-styles change, every tested page shows its main content
in about 1 s on a throttled phone except the homepage (3.3 s): its main
element is the stage photo, which the browser found only after reading
the stylesheet now inside the HTML.

- BaseLayout takes a preloadImage prop and announces it in <head>
  (imagesrcset + fetchpriority high), before the font preloads.
- Used by the homepage and the leg hubs (stage photo). Article and Expo
  hub pages paint text first and get no image preload.
- Change log BB-CHG-0067.
