Drop the link-for-page trade; publish the Expo contract award ledger

Outcome of a two-round external strategy review of the proposed "email
companies, give them a free page, ask for a backlink" engine. The engine is
not being built, and the trade behind it is withdrawn from the site.

Why: the exchange is link spam under both Google's spam policies (services
for links, widget links) and Bing's guidelines — and we were advertising it
openly, with /for-businesses saying the widget's attribution link "is the
license". Separately, 11 of our 12 contact emails sit in prior-consent
jurisdictions (DE, AT, CH, IT, JP, RS); the 12th is the US State Department.
There is no lawful list to run a campaign against.

WITHDRAWN AND FIXED (BB-CHG-0015)

- Widget links are now rel="nofollow noopener". Embedding is unconditional
  and the credit link is removable. Zero embeds exist, so nothing was lost.
- The outreach templates are editorial correction notices now: no offer, no
  claim pitch, no link request. Deleted the line "Many delegations use pages
  like this" — zero listings have ever been claimed, so it was false in the
  first sentence of a cold email. Deleted the fallback that called
  contractors and agencies "exhibitors"; there are no exhibitor listings.
- Opt-out finally suppresses. The admin was still rendering a ready-to-send
  draft and a mailto for people who had asked us to stop.
- The privacy notice claimed "we do not collect names, accounts, or payment
  details" while listing masters hold named business contacts and /manage
  grants access. Rewritten to describe what actually happens, plus the right
  to correction, deletion and no-contact.
- Removed the "you start from ours" authority claim; the page now states the
  real scale.
- Growth plan: invented forecast deleted, Resend reframed as owner
  notifications only, day-7 follow-up removed, "ask for a link back" turned
  into a standing rule never to do it.

SECURITY

- /manage?token=<64-hex> loaded GA4, whose page_location is the full URL —
  the magic-link token would have gone to Google. BaseLayout now honours
  meta.noAnalytics; /manage sets it and Referrer-Policy: no-referrer. No
  tokens have ever been issued, so nothing leaked.
- Manage saves committed photos on a token checked only against the
  build-time snapshot. assertTokenFresh() now validates against the live
  file before any write.

NEW: THE CONTRACT LEDGER (BB-CHG-0016)

/expo-2027/contracts — who is being paid to build and run Expo 2027, in one
comparable table, plus /data/expo-2027-contracts.json. Seven packages, each
verified against Serbian press today: collective pavilions (Sigong Tech, RSD
5.28bn, 6 bidders), ceremonies (Balich Wonder Studio, RSD 2.32bn), logistics
(Intereuropa), Village laundry, site supervision (Arhi Nova), Village
accommodation operator (Aurion + Delta), ticket printing (Zavod Topčider).

The discipline is the product: consortium awards counted once with every
member named, subcontractor value never added to the prime, framework
ceilings labelled as ceilings, sources and a verification date on every row,
and deliberately no total — an incomplete ledger cannot produce one honestly.
Scope is the organiser's procurement; national pavilions are excluded and
said to be.

Also corrected: the participant tracker dated Slovakia's first participation
contract to 15 Nov 2025 sourced to Wikipedia. The organiser's own
announcement says 12 Nov 2025, now the source.

New globals.css pattern c-fineprint added before use, per the Design Law.
