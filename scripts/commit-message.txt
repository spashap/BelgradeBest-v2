submit-to-bing: retry Bing's transient UnknownError, not just throttles

Running --all --fresh over the full 156-URL sitemap exposed a hole in the
submitter. Bing answers HTTP 400 with an ErrorCode envelope rather than a real
status, and the script only retried ErrorCode 5 (ThrottleHost). ErrorCode 2
("UnknownError") is also transient — the same URL that failed with it during the
sweep had resolved cleanly minutes earlier — but it fell through to the hard-error
path, was reported UNKNOWN, and was therefore skipped by --fresh. Since --fresh
deliberately errs toward skipping rather than re-submitting, a flaky sweep
silently under-submits. Both codes are now retried with backoff.

The runbook gains the practical rule that follows: use --all --fresh to DISCOVER
the gap, then submit the named URLs with --prefix, which performs no per-URL
status lookup and so cannot be thinned by Bing's error rate.

That is how the 10 remaining never-crawled URLs were submitted on 2026-09-13 —
/areas/novi-beograd, /areas/skadarlija and eight /glossary/ terms including
/glossary/bie (Bureau International des Expositions), Expo vocabulary that was
invisible on the channel delivering effectively all of the site's organic clicks.
Accepted; quota 89 -> 79 daily, 1789 -> 1779 monthly, 21 URLs submitted in total
today across this and the German set.
