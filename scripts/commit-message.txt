Affiliates admin with on/off switches and click stats; articles reshaped

New /admin/affiliates page: one row per partner (Booking.com, Welcome
Pickups, Air Serbia via CJ; aloSIM shown as not available) with its ID or
tracked link and an On/Off switch. A save commits src/data/affiliates.json
and the Vercel rebuild switches the public boxes in about 1-2 minutes; the
public pages stay static and gain no runtime requests. lib/affiliates.ts
resolves every block at build time; the old per-target switches are gone.

Clicks send a GA4 event named aff_click_<partner> from a small inline
listener that is emitted only on pages carrying a box, so the admin can
show clicks by partner, page (with click rate), country, device and
source with no GA4 setup. The privacy page says so.

Articles: airport-to-city gains "When is a pre-booked transfer worth it?"
(the free bus stays the first answer), taxis gains a tout-avoidance note,
Expo getting-there gains "Flying to Belgrade for Expo 2027" in English,
German and Slovak, and Expo where-to-stay gains search advice. No prices
or terms that could not be verified.
