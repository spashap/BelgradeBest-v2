Expo portal: rebuild /expo-2027 as a data-rich two-persona hub; retire /participants into it (301)

The generic leg hub never surfaced the section's best assets (pavilions,
tracker, countdown, corporate area), and the participants article hand-copied
tracker numbers that drifted stale. /expo-2027 is now a one-off portal page
(same Warm Editorial tokens, unique structure) whose every number renders
from the data masters at build.

New src/pages/expo-2027/index.astro:
- Countdown band (days-to-opening computed at build, refreshed live by JS,
  flips to days-left during the event) + key facts + two persona CTAs.
- Sticky anchor sub-nav; persona tabs (visitor / participant) that show-hide
  sections with JS and degrade to plain anchors without it.
- Visitor path: leg hero + intro, the 6 article cards, cross-leg trip rail
  (where-to-stay, food, visit, plan), good-to-know facts, unknowns box.
- Who's coming: live stats (139 official / 93 named / continents / profiles),
  honest two-numbers note, latest milestones -> tracker.
- Countries A-Z: all 93 named countries in a searchable, region-filterable,
  sortable table (filter bar ships [hidden]; no-JS users get the full A-Z
  table), each linked to its pavilion profile; orgs section (CERN, Posta
  Srbije, World Expo Museum).
- Participant path: how-participation-works explainer folded from the
  retired article (counts interpolated, BIE/visa/three-zones), directory/
  tracker/corporate-area cards, gold claim-your-profile note.

  breadcrumb + Event + FAQPage.
