Entry pages: passport rules corrected, proof of funds added (EN + DE)

The visa-and-entry pages said the Serbian MFA requires a passport valid
90 days after departure, two blank pages and issued within 10 years.
Checked 17 Nov against the sources: those three are the MFA's conditions
for a visa APPLICATION. For visa-free visitors the MFA's general entry
requirements (and the government's Welcome to Serbia portal) ask only
for a valid travel document. The 90 days is the UK government's advice;
Germany's Foreign Office and Canada say valid for the whole stay.

- Passport section rewritten on /plan-your-trip/visa-and-entry and its
  German twin: Serbia's own rule, what the UK, Germany and Canada
  advise, the safe plan (90 days covers all), and the visa-applicant
  conditions labelled as such.
- New short section on both pages: what else the border can ask for
  (EUR 50 a day in funds, recommended health insurance of EUR 20,000,
  documents such as a return ticket), from the MFA's entry requirements.
- English FAQ source note corrected so it cannot bring the claim back.
